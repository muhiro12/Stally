Keep a quiet record of the things and places that matter to you with Stally.

Bring names, notes, photos, and tags together in your library. Record everyday choices with Marks, keep periods of time together, and look back on your history. Dates can be exact, approximate, or left unknown. Archive items while keeping their records.

EVERYDAY USE
Use Stally on your device without a subscription.

OPTIONAL ICLOUD SYNC
An optional monthly auto-renewable subscription enables synchronization of your Stally library across your devices through your iCloud account. The subscription provides Stally's sync feature; it does not add iCloud storage.

After subscribing, turn on Use iCloud in Settings and relaunch Stally. Changes to the sync setting take effect the next time the app launches. Turning sync off or ending your subscription does not delete the records stored on your device.

The price for your country or region is shown before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.
